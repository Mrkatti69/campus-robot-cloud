==================================================
1. HOSTEL & MESS TIMINGS
==================================================
The campus hostel entry gates close strictly at 9:30 PM every night.
Students arriving after 9:30 PM must sign the late-entry register with the Chief Warden.
Hostel breakfast timing is from 7:30 AM to 9:00 AM on weekdays and 8:00 AM to 9:30 AM on Sundays.
Hostel lunch timing is from 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM.
Evening tea and snacks are served from 4:45 PM to 5:30 PM.
Hostel dinner timing is from 7:30 PM to 9:00 PM, and the mess closes strictly at 9:30 PM.

==================================================
2. CENTRAL LIBRARY & DIGITAL RESOURCE CENTER
==================================================
The timing of the campus library is from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM on all working days.
During semester examinations, the reading hall remains open until 11:00 PM.
Undergraduate students can borrow up to 4 books for 14 days using their smart ID card.
A late fine of 5 rupees per day is charged for overdue library books.
The Digital Library on the second floor provides free access to IEEE Xplore, Springer, and high-speed Wi-Fi.

==================================================
3. CAMPUS BUS & TRANSPORT ROUTES
==================================================
College buses operate across 6 major routes covering City Bus Stand, Railway Station, and Outer Ring Road.
Morning buses depart from the main city pickup points at 7:45 AM and 8:15 AM to reach campus by 8:50 AM.
Evening return buses depart from the Main Campus Gate at 4:45 PM and 5:30 PM.
Students must carry their valid transport bus pass while boarding.

==================================================
4. ACADEMIC BLOCKS & DEPARTMENT OFFICES
==================================================
Regular classes run from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM, Monday through Saturday, with a lunch break from 1:00 PM to 1:45 PM.
The Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) Department and HOD office are located in Block B, Second Floor, Room 204.
The Electronics and Communication (ECE) Department is located in Block A, First Floor, Room 108.
The Robotics and AI Innovation Lab is located in Block C, Ground Floor, Room 005, and is open to project students until 7:00 PM.
Minimum 75 percent attendance is mandatory in every subject to appear for end-semester university exams.

==================================================
5. ADMINISTRATIVE, FEE & SCHOLARSHIP OFFICE
==================================================
The Administrative Office and Accounts Counter are located in the Main Administrative Block, Ground Floor.
Fee payment and scholarship verification hours are from 10:00 AM to 3:30 PM, Monday to Friday.
Students applying for state or national scholarships must submit their income and caste certificates before the 15th of every month.
Bonafide certificates and ID card replacements are issued within 2 working days at Window Number 3.

==================================================
6. TRAINING & PLACEMENT CELL
==================================================
The Training and Placement Cell is situated in Block D, Third Floor.
Aptitude and coding training sessions are conducted every Wednesday and Friday from 3:00 PM to 4:30 PM.
Students with a CGPA of 6.5 and above with no active backlogs are eligible for Tier-1 campus recruitment drives.

==================================================
7. MEDICAL CENTER & EMERGENCY CONTACTS
==================================================
The Campus Health and First-Aid Center is located near the Main Gate and operates 24/7 with a resident nurse.
A visiting doctor is available daily from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM and 4:00 PM to 6:00 PM.
Campus Security Control Room Emergency Helpline: Extension 100 or +91-98765-43210.
Anti-Ragging Committee 24/7 Helpline: Extension 108 or +91-98765-43211.
